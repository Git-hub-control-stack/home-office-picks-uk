Affiliate Disclosure

How we make money and how it affects our recommendations.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you.

We work with partner retailers through the Awin affiliate network (for example furniture and ergonomics brands that sell direct), and we are a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.co.uk. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Links to affiliate retailers are marked rel="sponsored" in the page code and each guide carries a disclosure line.

Editorial independence

Our recommendations are based on published specifications (height ranges, load ratings, recline angles, VESA patterns, microphone counts and the like), brand track record, warranty terms, verification of every link against the live retailer page, and value for money. Where we own a product we say so and show it. Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we recommend, which retailer we link first, or how we score them: we pick the best product for the category first, then link it. No retailer or brand pays for placement.

Pricing and availability

We do not quote prices in our write-ups. Where a price appears in a comparison table it is taken from the retailer's own product data feed and shows the date it was read. Prices and stock change constantly, so always check the current price on the retailer's page before purchasing. We aim to update pages regularly but cannot guarantee real-time accuracy. We never display Amazon prices, ratings or stock status.
