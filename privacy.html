Privacy

Simple, minimal.

What we collect

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run a login system, we do not have comment forms, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly. The only thing the site stores in your browser is your light/dark theme preference, kept locally and never sent anywhere.

Third parties

Your browser, your network and the hosting provider may record standard technical data (such as IP address and pages requested) under their own policies. When you follow an affiliate link, the destination retailer or the affiliate network (Awin, Amazon) may set cookies or add tracking parameters to attribute the referral to this site; that processing is governed by their privacy policies, not ours.

Your rights

For any privacy question or request relating to this site, email hello@homesetuppicks.co.uk. Because we hold no personal data about visitors, most requests will be answered by pointing you to the relevant third party.
